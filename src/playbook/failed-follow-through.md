## What it looks like

A stock pushes out the gate and hits a prior key, then pulls hard.  The stock then hits a mid-point for an entry, then continues failing.  Thus the name - failing to follow through.

## Entry

Enter on the mid-point of the pull, or when the stock continues to break down.

## Where I'm wrong

Your initial stop out is near the high of day (HOD).  Once confirmed, the next stop out is if the stock clears and holds the mid-point.  

## Targets

Generally, holding to VWAP is ideal.  Goal is 4:1 risk/reward.  If you're feeling randy, you can hold for a key line below VWAP.
