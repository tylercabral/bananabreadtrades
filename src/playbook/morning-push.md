## What it looks like

A stock gapping up (5%+) and breaking out near key daily levels.  Play occurs at open.

## Entry

Enter on the candle that hits the key as long as there is strong volume and momentum.

## Where I'm wrong

Any fails (10-20 cents) and a stock pulling hard at the key level.

## Targets

Gap up breakouts (or sometimes referred to as Episodic Pivots) are good multi-day plays.  I like to take off profits at the 4:1 risk/reward level and you can trail-stop the rest.
