## What it looks like

Small cap runner on big volume (25 million+).  Pulls back and consolidates on the second day on small volume (liquidity dries up).  Look for a push on the 3rd day towards previous day highs.

## Entry

Enter on a break of the previous day highs or key line.

## Where I'm wrong

Any fails at the key line or entry.

## Targets

Any big exhaustion, look for exits.
