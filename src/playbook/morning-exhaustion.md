## What it looks like

Overextended charts that are pushing in the morning, towards key levels.  Pattern occurs in the first 30 minutes of market opening and is purely a scalp.  Smaller, reduced version of the Failed Follow Through.

## Entry

Second candle after hitting key and slowing down.  Stock should exhibit signs of slowdown after a strong morning into a key level.  

## Where I'm wrong

Any movement beyond entry (10-20 cents).

## Targets

As this is a scalp, any hard pulls should be taken for profit.  VWAP and key levels below should be kept in mind as well as exit points.  Not a trade to marry, purely a quick in/out.
