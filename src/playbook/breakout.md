## What it looks like

A stock in a clear uptrend on the daily chart.  The stock then has a strong open below the key line with momentum.  The distance from open to key line is generally 5% or less.  A gap of 5% or less is acceptable.

## Entry

The stock should clear the key line, pull, hold the key line, then breakout of the prior few candles.  Breakouts generally occur in the morning session.

## Where I'm wrong

I'm out if the stock fails to hold the key line, or 10-20 cents below. 

## Targets

Strong breakouts usually preceed a multi-day run.  I'll always take some off after hitting a 4:1 risk/reward ratio.
