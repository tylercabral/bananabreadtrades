## What it looks like

Small cap stocks gapping up on the day or experiencing a large run.  The stock is generally broken (exhaust out premarket or on the day prior) and is pushing in the morning.

## Entry

Any exhaustion into key levels.

## Where I'm wrong

If the stock continues moving past key levels, or 10-20 cents beyond entry.

## Targets

Whole or half-dollar marks for exits.
